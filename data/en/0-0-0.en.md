On July 14, 1868, we, the undersigned state peasants of the village (khutor) of Kriusha, Samoylovka Volost, Balashov District, Saratov Province, were assembled at the village gathering and gave this resolution to the reserve forester, Sub-Lieutenant Mr. Bogdanov, to the effect that 6 desyatinas and 850 square sazhens of land, which was not listed in either our land allotment or our forest allotment, we accept into the composition of our land holdings under quitrent (obrok), on equal terms with lands of the same kind, for the sum of 2 rubles 20 kopecks in silver, due according to the valuation for the forest material located on the above-mentioned area; and we pledge to pay it, to which we set our signatures.

Village elder (starosta) of the khutor of Kriusha, Mikhail Nikitchenkov. And on behalf of him, being illiterate and holding no office of any kind, his fellow villager Foma Yakovenkov set his hand.
